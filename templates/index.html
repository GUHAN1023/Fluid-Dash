Role Switcher & Permissions Guide

Test how the portal isolates permissions and changes views across user roles.

👑 Switch to Organizer ⚖️ Switch to Judge A ⚖️ Switch to Judge B 💻 Switch to Participant 👁️ Switch to Visitor
👑 Organizer Role Full administrative access. Can access the live judging dashboard, view all scores, and download full CSV exports.
⚖️ Judge Role (A / B) Assigned to review projects and submit rubric scores. Strictly blocked by the backend from viewing peer judges' scores (HTTP 403).
💻 Participant Role Can manage team membership and view project status. Blocked by the backend from judge scoring endpoints (HTTP 403).
👁️ Public Visitor Can browse the public gallery, participate in community voting (T3), and query public REST API endpoints (T4).
